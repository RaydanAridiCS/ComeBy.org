# Security policy

Comeby holds guests' names, phone numbers and emails, so we take security seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue.** Email **security@comeby.org** with:

- what you found and where,
- steps to reproduce,
- the impact you think it has.

We'll acknowledge within 72 hours and keep you updated until it's fixed. We're happy to credit you once the fix ships.

## Scope

The hosted service at comeby.org and this repository. Please don't run automated scanners against comeby.org, access other people's data, or disrupt the service.
